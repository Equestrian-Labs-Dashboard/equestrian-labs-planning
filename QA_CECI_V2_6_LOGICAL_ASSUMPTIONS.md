# Strategic Operating Model v2.6 — Logical assumptions and no-placeholder pass

- Removed visible `Actuals pending` defaults from Retention, Purchasing and Capital Efficiency.
- Incremental Ad Spend is phased across 2026–2029 using a front-loaded curve tied to Funding Date; it is not copied flat.
- 2029 Total Ad Spend includes base spend, phased incremental spend and the editable reinvestment amount.
- Dover market opportunity grows 5% annually; capture increases and overlap declines gradually.
- 2026 Organic Growth contributes $0 to 2026 revenue and becomes the 2027 growth assumption.
- Purchasing defaults use last validated actuals and logical editable targets. Connected actuals overwrite current/2026 when available.
- Operations defaults are non-zero, logical and editable; they affect GP2, GP3, EBITDA and Cash Flow.
- All future assumptions remain editable and persist by Draft, Budget, Forecast and Board in localStorage.
